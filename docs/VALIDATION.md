## AI model (supervised)

A gradient-boosting classifier (scikit-learn `HistGradientBoostingClassifier`) learns which feature combinations match the labelled scenarios; SHAP explains each player's score. Every player is scored by a model that never saw them (5-fold out-of-fold), and the result enters the score as one more reason (`ML_MODEL`: +10 at probability 0.5, +20 at 0.85), so it is explained like every other flag.

Out-of-fold ROC-AUC **0.999**, average precision **0.991**, recall at probability 0.5 **95%** with **0.10%** of normal players above 0.5.

Most influential features (mean |SHAP|): deposits vs income, betting turnover vs deposits, stake vs own baseline, deposits vs peers, average stake vs peers, average stake.

## Thresholds and weights in use

Bands: Low 0-39, Medium 40-69, High 70-89, Critical 90-100 (alert at 40+). Rule thresholds and points live in `THRESHOLDS` and `build_reasons()` in `backend/pipeline/detect.py`; the peer z threshold is 3.0 (upward deviations only, robust z on a log scale). Compliance indicators use the $10,000 24-hour total (`LARGE_TRANSACTION_24H`) and deposits of $8,000-$9,999.99 or split deposits crossing $10,000 (`STRUCTURING`).

## Known limits

- The data and the detectors were designed by the same team, so these numbers show the pipeline works end to end; they are not evidence of real-world performance.
- The legacy-rules comparison depends on how many legitimate high-value players the generator plants (`steady_high_value`, 1% of normal players). These are the players fixed "deposit over $X" rules mis-rank; with none of them, both orderings tie at the top.
- Peer deviation is the hardest type: a single metric far above peers scores 25-40 points on its own, so some injected players stay just under the alert threshold. Players who barely bet in the current window cannot be caught by 30-day features at all.
- The AI model learns the planted scenarios, so its scores here are optimistic even out-of-fold: it has seen other players of the same scenario. In production it would be trained on investigator-confirmed outcomes and monitored for drift.
